# Plan

Read and follow `../optim-plans/SKILL.md`, treating the request as if it named `plan`.

Depth: `plan` means 1 to 5 planning questions, at most three refinement rounds, a 600 second reviewer/criticizer timeout, and at most three high-priority comments or questions per round.
