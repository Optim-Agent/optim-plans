# Big Plan

Read and follow `../optim-plans/SKILL.md`, treating the request as if it named `big-plan`.

Depth: `big-plan` means 5 to 10 planning questions, required brainstorming websearch, at most five refinement rounds, an 1800 second reviewer/criticizer timeout, and at most five high-priority comments or questions per round.
